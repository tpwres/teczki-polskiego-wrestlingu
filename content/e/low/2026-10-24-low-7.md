What's in the Box? was a show by [Legacy of Wrestling](@/o/low.md), and the second Legacy event in Kraków in 2026. This time it was held in {{ map_pin(v="Klub Kwadrat") }}Klub Kwadrat. The name of the show is a quote from David Fincher's film [Seven][dobry-film-ale-przewidywalna-koncowka]. It was announced on 7.07.2026, three days before the [sixth Legacy event](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md).

## Build-up

* At the [last show](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md) [Max Speed](@/w/max-speed.md) challenged [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md) for the [Legacy of Wrestling European Championship](@/c/low-european-championship.md). Jakub initially declined, but ended up agreeing after all, intending to humiliate Speed.
* On 2.09.2026 [Shadow](@/w/shadow.md) announced his retirement from active wrestling; as a farewell to the fans, he decided to make final three appearances, including in Legacy.
* The match between Jakub and Max Speed was officially confirmed on 13.09.2026.
* On 16.09.2026 Shadow's opponent was revealed to be [KPW's](@/o/kpw.md) former [Championship](@/c/kpw-championship.md) and a fellow masked luchador: [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md).
* On 7.10.2026 came the announcement of a Tag Team Match between the [KPW Tag Team Champions](@/c/kpw-tag-team-championship.md) [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) & [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md), and [Boro](@/w/boro.md) teamed up with a mystery partner. At the [previous Legacy show](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md) Chemik won the [Legacy of Wrestling European Championship](@/c/low-european-championship.md) Contract by defeating Boro (among others), whereas the [recent KPW event](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) saw Haller aid Chemik in reaching for the tag team gold only because he didn't want to see the Wejherowo native possibly winning both team belts by his lonesome.
* At the [previous show](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md), when [Disco Pablo](@/w/disco-pablo.md) was being attacked by [Bartosz Plata](@/w/plata.md) and [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md), [Sambor](@/w/sambor.md) rushed to help. His assistance, however, ended up not making a difference and Sambor blamed Pablo for this defeat and his recent series of losses, turning on his friend. As a result, Pablo demanded a match against the Slavic warrior to which he agreed, but on the condition that the choice of the stipulation will be his. On 15.10.2026 Legacy officially confirmed the match, with the stipulation remaining a mystery.
* [Marcelito](@/w/marcelito.md), who also fell victim to Plata's betrayal, was announced on 19.10.2026 for a match against the traitorous former ally.
* On 21.10.2026 Legacy published a [video][ekstemum-lesne] in which Sambor revealed his choice of stipulation: Extreme Rules Match.
* A post made by the federation later that day stated that members of [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md) - [Tony Sheen](@/w/riverman.md), [Eryk Lesak](@/w/eryk-lesak.md) and [Arek Paterek](@/w/arek-paterek.md) - will appear at the show, with the first two taking part in a Tag Team Match.
* On 22.10 Boro posted a [reel][gra-wstepna], talking about his Tag Team Match against [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) & [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md). As he mentioned finding a Tag Team partner, Boro opened his bathrobe to show the [Tag Team T-shirt][koszulka-boruti] depicting him and his PpW/FRPW partner, [Mutant](@/w/mutant.md).

### Highlights

* The first match ended with a rare top-rope move by [Eryk Lesak](@/w/eryk-lesak.md) - a Splash onto [Antoni Ocean](@/w/antoni-ocean.md).
* After winning his match, [Shadow](@/w/shadow.md) was attacked with a Superkick from a mysterious figure posing for an audience member, who told him to "be careful what he wished for" before being removed from the ring by the staff. This unexpected interruption was caused by a former [MZW](@/o/mzw.md) wrestler [Lider](@/w/lider.md), whom Shadow named in an [interview][szedo-interwju] with [Center of Wrestling](@/o/cow.md) as somebody he'd like to have his [final match](@/e/mzw/2026-11-20-mzw-green-madness-ii.md) with.
* Before his match, [Disco Pablo](@/w/disco-pablo.md) patted down [Sędzia Seweryn](@/w/sedzia-seweryn.md) in search of foreign objects, but found none.
* Disco Pablo laid [Sambor](@/w/sambor.md) out on a club's table some fans were sitting at. He then asked the fans to join hands with him and do a wave, starting with the fan fartherst from him and ending with w powerful chop to the horizontal Sambor.
* Sambor produced a kendo stick, prompting the fans to chant "Stick it up your ass!". The stick was soon snatched from him by Pablo, causing the fans to chant "Stick it up his ass!" - which Pablo did.
* When Pablo pulled out a table from under the ring, the audience chanted "Stick it up his ass!". He didn't.
* A ladder found by Sambor was met with "Stick it up his ass!" chants. That didn't happen either.
* After this match, a representative of [Polish Lobo][lobo-ale-nie-jablka] - a Kraków-based shop with Marvel/DC/horror/anime figurines, and the show's sponsor - joined [Wiktor](@/w/wiktor-longman.md) in the ring to give the winner of an online competition his prize - a 1:1 replica of [Lobo's][bastich] hook. When asked what he was going to do with it, he didn't have the chance to answer before the audience started chanting "Stick it up your ass!".
* The [KPW Tag Team Champions](@/c/kpw-tag-team-championship.md) [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) and [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md) kept bickering throughout the match, still clearly not happy with having to be one another's partners and co-Champions.
* In a promo directed mostly at Chemik, [Boro](@/w/boro.md) introduced his Tag Team partner, who was "also very bald": [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md). Olgierd then rushed into the ring and laid waste to the KPW wrestlers, his theme song still playing.
* With Oskar Haller on the ropes, Boro produced a pink glove for his signature Oil Check, but it was snatched from him by Chemik.
* Later, Chemik took out the glove while Olgierd was on the ropes, seemingly with the same intention, but was stopped by Boro. Olgierd took the opportunity to pull down Chemik's trousers and promptly get on all fours, allowing Boro to push and trip Chemik over Olgierd's back and onto the ropes. The audience didn't fail to chant "Stick it up his ass!".
* Oskar Haller laughed at Chemik's misfortune along with the opposing team, before getting hit and thrown at the rope next to his uneasy partner. Olgierd then produced a pink glove of his own, put it on and - to the chants of "Stick it up his ass!" - performed a Double Oil Check on Chemik and Haller alongside Boro.
* Chemik broke Boro's pin on Haller, causing Olgierd to confront him. Chemik told Olgierd that he wasn't even legal, Olgierd replied that Chemik wasn't either - which the KPW Tag Team Champion countered with "I am barely legal!" (_Jestem ledwo legalny!_) - likely referring to [PpW's](@/o/ppw.md) biggest annual show.
* Olgierd then picked up Haller in a Fireman's Carry, telling Chemik "If you don't get your act together, I will kill him!" (_Jak się nie ogarniesz, to go zabiję!_). Chemik responded by picking up Boro and saying "Then I will kill him!" (_To ja zabiję jego!_). The stalemate ended with both Olgierd and Chemik throwing Haller and Boro to the ring (although not quite as lethally as expected).
