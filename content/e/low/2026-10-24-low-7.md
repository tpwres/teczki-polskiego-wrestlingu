What's in the Box? is an upcoming show by [Legacy of Wrestling](@/o/low.md), and the second Legacy event in Kraków in 2026. This time it will be held in {{ map_pin(v="Klub Kwadrat") }}Klub Kwadrat. The name of the show is a quote from David Fincher's film [Seven][dobry-film-ale-przewidywalna-koncowka]. It was announced on 7.07.2026, three days before the [sixth Legacy event](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md).

## Build-up

* At the [last show](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md) [Max Speed](@/w/max-speed.md) challenged [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md) for the [Legacy of Wrestling European Championship](@/c/low-european-championship.md). Jakub initially declined, but ended up agreeing after all, intending to humiliate Speed.
* On 2.09.2026 [Shadow](@/w/shadow.md) announced his retirement from active wrestling; as a farewell to the fans, he decided to make final three appearances, including in Legacy.
* The match between Jakub and Max Speed was officially confirmed on 13.09.2026.
* On 16.09.2026 Shadow's opponent was revealed to be [KPW's](@/o/kpw.md) former [Championship](@/c/kpw-championship.md) and a fellow masked luchador: [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md).
* On 7.10.2026 came the announcement of a Tag Team Match between the [KPW Tag Team Champions](@/c/kpw-tag-team-championship.md) [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) & [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md), and [Boro](@/w/boro.md) teamed up with a mystery partner. At the [previous Legacy show](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md) Chemik won the [Legacy of Wrestling European Championship](@/c/low-european-championship.md) Contract by defeating Boro (among others), whereas the [recent KPW event](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) saw Haller aid Chemik in reaching for the tag team gold only because he didn't want to see the Wejherowo native possibly winning both team belts by his lonesome.
