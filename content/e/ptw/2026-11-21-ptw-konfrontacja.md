"Konfrontacja" (_The Confrontation_) was a show by [Prime Time Wrestling](@/o/ptw.md). Initially it was announced under the name "Cena Chwały" (_The Price of Glory_).

## Build-up

* At the [previous show](@/e/ptw/2026-10-31-ptw-duch-przeszlosci.md), a stipulation for a [PTW Championship](@/c/ptw-championship.md) Match between [Spartan](@/w/spartan.md) and [Vincent Caravaggio](@/w/vincent-caravaggio.md) was decided by Chairman [Łukasz Okoński](@/w/lukasz-okonski.md). He announced a "Do trzech razy sztuka" (lit. _third time's the charm_) match, where the winner needs to "defeat the opponent three times". This likely makes it a "3 out of 5 falls" match, but as per [Okoński's Q&A][live], the exact rules will only be shared by the referees at a later date.
* A [video][okon-zapowiada] published on 4.11.2026 disclosed early details of the show: a [PTW Championship](@/c/ptw-championship.md) Match between [Spartan](@/w/spartan.md) and [Vincent Caravaggio](@/w/vincent-caravaggio.md), [Disco Pablo](@/w/disco-pablo.md) returning to PTW's ring in a match against ["Starboy" Nano Lopez](@/w/nano-lopez.md), and the next four Golden Ticket Tournament entrants: adeptka Paula (shortly thereafter renamed Valentina Vex) vs [Sigma Boy xD](@/w/sigma-boy.md), and [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md) vs [Conte](@/w/conte.md).
* Valentina Vex vs [Sigma Boy xD](@/w/sigma-boy.md) was officially announced on 11.11.2026, and [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md) vs [Conte](@/w/conte.md) the day after.
* On 12.11.2026 PTW announced a Tag Team Match between The Lancadores (M.R.D. & Kenny B.) vs Synowie Lasu ([Polski Niedźwiedź](@/w/polski-niedzwiedz.md) & [Misiek](@/w/misiek.md)).
* On 13.11.2026 the match between Nano Lopez and Disco Pablo was officially confirmed and upgraded to a [PTW Underground Championship](@/c/ptw-underground-championship.md) Match.
* Later the same day came the announcement of [CJ Klakson](@/w/cj-klakson.md) vs Damian Miller.
* On 14.11.2026 the match between Conte and Whiteblade was changed to a Tag Team Match between First Class ([El G](@/w/el-g.md) & [Conte](@/w/conte.md)) and [Proroctwo](@/tt/proroctwo.md) ([Syriusz Dziedzic](@/w/dziedzic.md) & [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md)). To some confusion, it was announced that any member of the winning team is eligible to Golden Ticket ladder match entry, though El G already won his at the previous show.
* A few hours later [Kalom](@/w/kalom.md) vs Pierre was announced.
* On 15.11.2026 the Championship match between Spartan and Caravaggio was officially confirmed. This will be Caravaggio's second attempt at the title, following his loss to Spartan at [Koloseum](@/e/ptw/2026-04-18-ptw-koloseum.md).

### Notes

* Gwiezdny Marek demanded to be added into a match between two other Lucky 8 winners: [Kalom](@/w/kalom.md) and Pierre. Chairman obliged, but in a tricky way, adding him as a special guest referee, Sędzia Marek.
* As Marek was continuosly sabotaging the match with bad officiating, Kalom and Pierre were both counted out outside the ring. As he grabbed the mic, he continued to berate the whole Lucky 8 cast and fed up Okoński made a rematch, including Marek, for a next show.
* Former rookie Paula, now known as Valentina Vex, debuted a new, goth-styled look, including a full facepaint. [Sigma Boy xD](@/w/sigma-boy.md), initially reluctant to fight his former pupil, beat her with a roll-up. Post-match, Dziedzic and Whiteblade entered the ring, circling Sigma, but Okoński stopped the attack in its tracks.
* Valentina's debut marks a significant milestone in PTW history. In 5 years of PTW, she is the first female homegrown rookie to make a full in-ring debut.
* Bruised and beaten after their blood feud, Daniel Razor and Fabian entered the ring with a set table. A face to face, moderated by Resivny, quickly devolved from war of words into physical form, ending with Fabian putting Razor through the table - to the surprise of nobody, except from Resivny.
* Martusia made her manager entrance wearing a custom t-shirt, spelling 'Martusia'.
* Per the stipulation of tag team match, winning team can choose a member to advance into Golden Ticket Tournament ladder match. Proroctwo chooses Whiteblade. El G, though part of the losing team, did not lose the spot earned previously
* _Do Trzech Razy Sztuka_ match for PTW Championship turned out to be an ordinary Three out of Five Falls match. Results breakdown:
   1. Spartan scores a pinfall with a Spartan kick (1:0) 
   2. Spartan scores a second pinfall with a Spartan bomb (2:0)
   3. Vincent scores a point via disqualification after Spartan uses the  PTW Championship (2:1)
   4. Vincent scores another point with a pinning combination (2:2)
   5. Vincent scores a third point with 3 straight instances of Szlachecka Szarża, thus winning a whole match and the PTW Championship (2:3).
