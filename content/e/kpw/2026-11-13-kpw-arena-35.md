KPW Arena 35: Piątek 13 (_Friday the 13th_) was a show by [Kombat Pro Wrestling](@/o/kpw.md).

## Build-up

* At the end of [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) [Krystian Malinowski](@/w/krystian-malinowski.md) was forcibly put in a straitjacket by [Michał Fux](@/w/michal-fux.md), [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md), [Leon Lato](@/w/leon-lato.md) and [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md), and removed from the premises. On 4.10.2026 KPW published a [video][malinowskiego-wypuscili] showing Chairman Malinowski - now much calmer and cheerful - leaving a psychiatric hospital in Gdańsk, his villainous persona seemingly cured. Two weeks later, on 17.10.2026, Malinowski was announced to make a statement addressing the recent events.
* KPW began announcing the show's match-ups on 20.10.2026. In keeping with the name of the event, and the date not being too far from Halloween, the first published pair of contenders was [Greg](@/w/greg.md) vs [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md) in a Hardcore Horror Match.
* The second match, announced on 26.10.2026, was [Markus Paskal](@/w/markus-paskal.md) vs [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md).
* At [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) Adept Dwa won the KPW Contract Three-Way Match and officially joined the roster. On 28.10.2026 KPW revealed the ring name chosen by the former rookie: [Szymon Dragan](@/w/szymon-dragan.md); at the same time it was announced that his mettle will be tested by Erik Asmus, last seen at [Arena 32](@/e/kpw/2026-03-13-kpw-arena-32.md).
* On 31.10.2026 KPW announced a 3 vs 3 Elimination Match. On the one team will be [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md), [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md) and Nico Narciso (last seen at [Arena 32](@/e/kpw/2026-03-13-kpw-arena-32.md)), on the other: [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md), [Michał Fux](@/w/michal-fux.md) and [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md). Olgierd had previously appeared at ([Arena 34](@/e/kpw/2026-06-26-kpw-arena-34.md)) in a match against Filip Fux, the [Fux brothers](@/tt/bracia-fux.md) faced each other at [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md), and the relationship between Chemik and Haller has been strained since the two won the [KPW Tag Team Championship](@/c/kpw-tag-team-championship.md) at the same show.
* On 2.11.2026 KPW informed that [Sam Della Valle](@/w/sam-rogers.md) challenged [Eryk Lesak](@/w/eryk-lesak.md) for his [KPW OldTown Championship](@/c/kpw-old-town-championship.md). Della Valle had previous encounters with some of KPW's wrestlers at [Legacy of Wrestling: Enter the Dragon](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md).
* The final match, announced on 4.11.2026, was ??? vs ??? - a free spot on the card open to anyone "brave enough to take advantage of it".

### Highlights

* The weapons used in the Hardcore Horror Match included: a kendo stick, a steel chair, a steel chain, a ladder, two light tubes, a [B-41 road sign][zakaz-wstepu] _no entry for pedestrians_ ([previously](@/e/kpw/2026-01-16-kpw-arena-31.md) used by [Ron Corvus](@/w/ron-corvus.md)), a ukulele, a trashcan lid, a baseball bat and a TV (different from the one seen at [Arena 29](@/e/kpw/2025-06-20-kpw-arena-29.md)).
* After the match the lights went out and four masked figures in habits similar to [Greg's](@/w/greg.md) entered the ring, surrounding the self-proclaimed pope. After an apparent blessing they attacked Greg, dropping the masks to reveal themselves as [Hans Schulte](@/w/hans-schulte.md), Erik Asmus, Friedrich Pohlmann and [Veit Müller](@/w/veit-mueller.md) Hans cut a short promo deriding Greg, then the German quartet continued to dish out more punishment, using a leather belt and an electric taser. Suddenly to Greg's aid came [David Oliwa](@/w/david-oliwa.md), not seen since [Arena 31](@/e/kpw/2026-01-16-kpw-arena-31.md), turning face in the process to large audience pop. David claimed a spot in the ??? vs ??? match, saying he was ready to fight; the Germans chose Friedrich Pohlmann to go against Oliwa.
* [Sam Della Valle](@/w/sam-rogers.md) accidentally knocked out [Krystian Czekaj](@/w/krystian-czekaj.md) with a Clothesline when [Eryk Lesak](@/w/eryk-lesak.md) swiftly moved out of the way. Lesak used this opportunity to cheat by clobbering his opponent with the [KPW OldTown Championship](@/c/kpw-old-town-championship.md), then grabbed the referee by the belt and carried him towards the downed Italian for a three-count.
* Oliwa fought wearing a brace on his left leg, until it was removed by Pohlmann for an unfair advantage.
* [Krystian Malinowski](@/w/krystian-malinowski.md) presented himself to the audience as a new man, now fully cured and having reached nirvana. He was soon joined in the ring by [Rosetti](@/w/rosetti.md) who countered the Chairman's claims by playing the full version of his [video][malinowskiego-wypuscili], which included a portion previously unseen - that of Malinowski cursing numerous KPW wrestlers, including [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md). Rosetti then declared Malinowski unfit for duty as a Chairman and, in accordance with the rules put in place during democratic elections at [Arena 13](@/e/kpw/2019-04-05-kpw-arena-13.md), relieved him of his position which he immediately took over himself. Malinowski promptly tried to fire Rosetti, but it was too late and he no longer held any power. A security member (Adept Cztery), called into the ring by Malinowski to remove Rosetti, ended up removing Malinowski on Rosetti's orders instead.
* [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) hit [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md) with his own moves: the Crocodile Roll followed shortly by Ostatnie Namaszczenie.
* Chemik and Haller eliminated each other at the same time, layig on the ring side by side with their hands on one another, which [Krystian Czekaj](@/w/krystian-czekaj.md) counted as a double pin.
* Order of eliminations:
  1. Chemik and Oskar Haller - double elimination
  2. Nico Narciso
  3. Olgierd
  4. Filip Fux
* When closing the show, [Kinga Miotke](@/w/kinga-miotke.md) announced the date of the [next show](@/e/kpw/2027-01-08-kpw-arena-36.md), set to take place a week before [WWE's return to Poland](@/e/wwe/2027-01-16-wwe-road-to-royal-rumble.md).
