KPW Arena 35: Piątek 13 (_Friday the 13th_) is an upcoming show by [Kombat Pro Wrestling](@/o/kpw.md).

## Build-up

* At the end of [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) [Krystian Malinowski](@/w/krystian-malinowski.md) was forcibly put in a straitjacket by [Michał Fux](@/w/michal-fux.md), [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md), [Leon Lato](@/w/leon-lato.md) and [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md), and removed from the premises. On 4.10.2026 KPW published a [video][malinowskiego-wypuscili] showing Chairman Malinowski - now much calmer and cheerful - leaving a psychiatric hospital in Gdańsk, his villainous persona seemingly cured. Two weeks later, on 17.10.2026, Malinowski was announced to make a statement addressing the recent events.
* KPW began announcing the show's match-ups on 20.10.2026. In keeping with the name of the event, and the date not being too far from Halloween, the first published pair of contenders was [Greg](@/w/greg.md) vs [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md) in a Hardcore Horror Match.
* The second match, announced on 26.10.2026, was [Markus Paskal](@/w/markus-paskal.md) vs [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md).
* At [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) Adept Dwa won the KPW Contract Three-Way Match and officially joined the roster. On 28.10.2026 KPW revealed the ring name chosen by the former rookie: [Szymon Dragan](@/w/szymon-dragan.md); at the same time it was announced that his mettle will be tested by Erik Asmus, last seen at [Arena 32](@/e/kpw/2026-03-13-kpw-arena-32.md).
