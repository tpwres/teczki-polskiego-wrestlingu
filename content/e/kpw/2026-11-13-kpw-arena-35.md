KPW Arena 35: Piątek 13 (_Friday the 13th_) is an upcoming show by [Kombat Pro Wrestling](@/o/kpw.md).

## Build-up

* At the end of [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) [Krystian Malinowski](@/w/krystian-malinowski.md) was forcibly put in a straitjacket by [Michał Fux](@/w/michal-fux.md), [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md), [Leon Lato](@/w/leon-lato.md) and [Zefir](@/w/zefir.md), and removed from the premises. On 4.10.2026 KPW published a [video][malinowskiego-wypuscili] showing Chairman Malinowski - now much calmer and cheerful - leaving a psychiatric hospital in Gdańsk, his vaillainous persona seemingly cured.
