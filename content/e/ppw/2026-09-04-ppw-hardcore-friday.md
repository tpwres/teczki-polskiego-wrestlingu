Harcore Friday: Żeby Było Ci Dobrze (roughly _To Make You Feel Good_) is an upcoming show by [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md). Like all previous Hardcore Friday shows, it will also be set in [2KOŁA Motorcycle Club](@/v/2kola.md).

## Build-up

* On 27.07.2026 PpW announced 10 wrestlers without match-ups: [Gabriel Queen](@/w/gabriel-queen.md), [Biesiad Strong](@/w/biesiad.md), [Johnny Blade](@/w/johnny-blade.md), [Laurance Roman](@/w/laurance-roman.md), [Zeritus](@/w/zeritus.md), Nick Schreier, [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md), [Oskar Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md), [Mutant](@/w/mutant.md) and [Mister Z](@/w/mister-z.md).
* The Hardcors (Johnny Blade & Biesiad Strong) had lost to [Rodzina](@/tt/rodzina.md) (represented by [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md) and [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md)) at [Lecimy Dalej](@/e/ppw/2026-05-30-ppw-lecimy-dalej.md). On 28.07.2026 PpW published a [video][biesiad-na-stole] of incensed Blade looking to get back at Rodzina and Biesiad challenging them to a fight. This resulted in a Tables Match between Biesiad and Golden being announced on 2.08.2026.
* On 3.09.2026 PpW posted a picture detailing a portion of the planned matches: [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md) vs [Biesiad Strong](@/w/biesiad.md); [Johnny Blade](@/w/johnny-blade.md) vs [Oskar Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md); [Zeritus](@/w/zeritus.md) vs [Boro](@/w/boro.md); and The Grind ([Laurance Roman](@/w/laurance-roman.md) & Nick Schreier) + [Gabriel Queen](@/w/gabriel-queen.md) vs [Zmowa](@/tt/zmowa.md) ([Mister Z](@/w/mister-z.md), [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md) & [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md)) in a 3-vs-3 Match.
