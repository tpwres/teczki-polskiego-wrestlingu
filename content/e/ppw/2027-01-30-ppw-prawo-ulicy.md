"Prawo Ulicy" (_Street Law_) is an upcoming wrestling event by [PpW Ewenement](@/o/ppw.md), to be held in [Mińska 65](@/v/minska-65.md). The event was announced during [Skok Na Kraków](@/e/ppw/2026-11-21-ppw-skok-na-krakow.md), in promos setting up matches, and directly by host [Michael HT](@/w/michael-ht.md).

## Build-up

* The event was announced during [Skok Na Kraków](@/e/ppw/2026-11-21-ppw-skok-na-krakow.md), both in challenges issued by competitors, and directly by host [Michael HT](@/w/michael-ht.md).
* On 27.11.2027 PpW announced the Ustawka Match 2, in which [Rodzina](@/tt/rodzina.md) ([Robert Star](@/w/robert-star.md) & [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md) & [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md) & [Oskar Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md) & [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md)) will go against Anty-Rodzina ([Kondzio the Cannonball](@/w/kondzio-the-cannonball.md) & [Boro](@/w/boro.md) & [Mutant](@/w/mutant.md) & [Biesiad Strong](@/w/biesiad.md) & [Johnny Blade](@/w/johnny-blade.md)). The match will be a culmination of several storylines that played out over the past several shows, both PpW's and [FRPW's](@/o/frpw.md), including Kondzio's kidnapping.
