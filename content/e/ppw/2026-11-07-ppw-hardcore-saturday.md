Hardcore Saturday was an event by [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md) that took place in [2KOŁA Motorcycle Pub](@/v/2kola.md) in Warsaw. This time the show was held on a Saturday instead of the usual Friday, hence the name.

## Build-up

* On 18.10.2026 PpW announced a Tag Team Match between Hungarians [Maverick](@/w/maverick.md) & [Sentinel](@/w/sentinel.md) and [Rodzina](@/tt/rodzina.md), represented by [Oskar Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md) & [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md).
* In a [video][sf-assassins-fist-i-tak-byl-lepszy] published on 25.10.2026 [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md) was given a Street Fight against [Biesiad Strong](@/w/biesiad.md). The match was officially confirmed the next day.
* On 28.10.2026 came the announcement of a Triple Threat Match: [Max Speed](@/w/max-speed.md) vs [Goblin](@/w/goblin.md) vs [Antoni Ocean](@/w/antoni-ocean.md).
* On 30.10.2026 PpW announced that [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md) and [Mister Z](@/w/mister-z.md) will confront each other regarding what happened between them at [Brutality](@/e/ppw/2026-10-10-ppw-brutality.md).
* A [video][product-placement-ciezkiego-sprzetu-budowlanego] released on 2.11.2026 showed [Gloria](@/w/gloria-g-szek.md) - disappointed with her loss at [Brutality](@/e/ppw/2026-10-10-ppw-brutality.md), at the hands of [Kalyana](@/w/kalyana.md) - agreeing to a mutually-beneficial partnership with [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md). The next day this agreement coalesced into the announcement of a Mixed Tag Team Match: Jakub & Gloria vs Kalyana & [Gabriel Queen](@/w/gabriel-queen.md).
* On 5.11.2026 PpW announced ["Ladykiller" Boro](@/w/boro.md) vs [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md), as an extended punishment for Rodzina's actions against [Kondzio the Cannonball](@/w/kondzio-the-cannonball.md) (see [Skok Na Kraków](@/e/ppw/2026-11-21-ppw-skok-na-krakow.md) for details).

### Highlights

* The show opened with the recent song by Reni Jusis, [_Fajnie że byłeś_](@/o/ppw.md#reni-jusis), whose music video features PpW talent.
* Host Michael HT named the show "Hardcore Friday, Saturday edition". When closing the show, he comically insisted that ["nothing is happening tomorrow"](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md) and urged the fans to stay at home and relax. He then proceeded to demonstrate one such method of relaxation, by laying down in the middle of the ring.
* Emil Völler unceremoniously inserted himself into the opening match, once the three original participant were ready to start. In a brief promo he claimed he received a phone call from "[Donald Tusk][lepszy-z-dwoch-donaldow], a fellow German" (Poland's sitting Prime Minister) who told him to "beat this Polish motherfucker's ass".
* The now-four-way match ended with Max and Goblin submitting Emil simultaneously - Max with an Ankle Lock, and Goblin with his Kabacki Krztusiec.
* In their segment, Mister Z acknowledged recent issues with Zmowa and asserted he always has a backup plan. Once Olgierd arrived, Zet apologised for often treating him like a brainless brute to do his dirty work.
  Trying to get Olgierd to re-join Zmowa, he offered a title match for the [PpW Championship](@/c/ppw-championship.md). Olgierd declined, stating that it wasn't on top of his mind. Faced with this, Zet gave him an ultimatum: either Olgierd accepts, or he's fired from PpW forever.
  Olgierd then called the bluff and proceeded listing his upcoming bookings: "tomorrow with [FRPW](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md), next week in Tricity with [KPW](@/e/kpw/2026-11-13-kpw-arena-35.md), two weeks on with [MZW](@/e/mzw/2026-11-20-mzw-green-madness-ii.md)", that he was waiting for [ShowOff](@/w/piotr-malecki.md) to reveal the next [Legacy](@/o/low.md) date, and made a reference to [Prezes Okoński](@/w/lukasz-okonski.md) still being in his [DMs][pm]. He then slagged PpW for being ran by a "banana" (slang for a _spoiled rich kid_) and an idiot, then quit on his own.
* Despite losing to Agatka, Monster High Suzie quickly recovered and attacked her post-match. This beatdown was however quickly interrupted by Julia Rave, who then aligned herself with Agatka. This was hinted at in Agatka's [reel][stylowka-na-hugo-weavinga] posted a week before, where she bumps into Julia by chance and the two compliment each other's choice of fashion.
* Isnorr came out carrying not only his signature axe, but also the rope that Rodzina used to restrain the captured Kondzio until his escape. The rope was then used as a weapon throughout the match, including the finisher which was a Camel Clutch plus a Rope Chokehold.
* After their match, Jakub attacked Gabriel Queen and put him out with his golden knuckles, then took the PpW Championship belt and posed with it, standing over the laid-out Queen. Commentary added that "what you see now in the ring, might be what you'll see by the time the [show in Kraków](@/e/ppw/2026-11-21-ppw-skok-na-krakow.md) ends".
* For the main event, Biesiad made his usual entrance through the crowd, ladder in hand. Before he even had a chance to open his customary beer, however, Filip attacked him, also coming through the venue's door. The action then spilled outside, into a chilly November night. The action outside included Filip going through a table set against a tree. Back inside, Biesiad finally had time for his brew, only for Filip to start choking him with his trouser belt. Later, an intervention by Isnorr stopped Biesiad from a top-rope maneuver. Johnny Blade, present in the bar area, jumped in to save his [Hardcors](@/tt/the-hardcors.md) partner, and with Boro's help managed to oust the red-headed lumberjack. However, Rodzina then sent in Oskar Alexander, whom Biesiad dispatched with a Buckshot Lariat, only to get pinned with a rollup by Filip.
* After his loss, a sad and pensive Biesiad remained in the ring, sitting against one of the corners and smoking a cigarette. Johnny Blade came to cheer him up with the fans' help.
