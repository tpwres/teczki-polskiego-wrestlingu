"Na Słowo Honoru" (_Word of Honour_) was a show by [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md), returning to [Teatr Komuna](@/v/teatr-komuna.md) in Warsaw.

## Build-up

* The first match, announced on 7.10.2026, was [Jacob Crane](@/w/jacob-crane.md) vs [Dieter Schwartz](@/w/dieter-schwartz.md) in a Word of Honour Match, which also gives the event its name. This stipulation is inspired by Ring of Honor's [Pure Wrestling Rules][roh-pure] - each wrestler is limited to at most three rope breaks, there is no time limit and the count-out is 20 seconds, instead of the usual 10.
* On 20.10.2026 FRPW announced a Mixed Tag Team Match between [Kalyana](@/w/kalyana.md) & [Max Speed](@/w/max-speed.md) and Kolektyw ([Gloria](@/w/gloria-g-szek.md) & [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md)).
* In a [video][emil-wxw] published on 22.10.2026, [Emil Völler](@/w/emil-voeller.md) issued an Open Challenge for his wXw Wrestling Academy Championship. Emil took the title from [Gabriel Queen](@/w/gabriel-queen.md) just a day before, during a wXw HEAT event in Gelsenkirchen.
* The next day the challenge was asnwered by [Goblin](@/w/goblin.md).
* On 28.10.2026 Asara was announced to debut in FRPW, in a match with [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md).
* On 30.10.2026 FRPW announced PSY.CHO, a team of ??? & [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md), without further details.
* 2.11.2026 saw the announcement of Quentin Carter & [Boro](@/w/boro.md) vs [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md) ([Bartosz Plata](@/w/plata.md) & [Tony Sheen](@/w/riverman.md)).
* On 4.11.2026 FRPW announced a match between two Oskars: [Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) & [Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md).
* [Markus Paskal](@/w/markus-paskal.md) vs [Antoni Ocean](@/w/antoni-ocean.md) was announced on 5.11.2026.

### Highlights

* Before the main show started, a minute of silence was held to acknowledge recent [tragic events][tragedia-w-krakowie] in Kraków. Just the day before, the authorities declared a period of [national mourning][zaloba] for Nov 8-9, which put the show at risk of cancellation.
* A new faction called PSY.CHO emerged, comprising of manager [Monster High Suzie](@/w/monster-high-suzie.md), [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md) and Stowarzysz, the latter now masked and wearing a straitjacket.
* Gustav Gryffin, billed as one of FRPW's founders and chairmen, came out to reveal plans for the next year, most importantly a tournament where the winner must face three opponents in a single evening. The exact ladder shall be established by qualifying matches, to take place at future shows. However, Emil Völler - the wXw Academy Champion - got a pass into the tournament.
* Immediately after this announcement, Kolektyw came out. Olgierd took the mic and asserted that he was the other finalist of the HeelTurn Invitational Tournament, and the only one able to compete (the other being the winner Leon Lato, currently injured). He then demanded not one, but three spots in the tournament: for himself, [Gloria](@/w/gloria-g-szek.md) and [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md). Gustav finally agreed to grant them one spot, the choice of person at their discretion, on the condition that they win their upcoming match.
