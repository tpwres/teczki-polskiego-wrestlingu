"Hardcore Friday" is name used by [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md) with a long tradition. As described by [PpW Wikia][wikia-hf], it was a backyard-era "weekly", which neither happened weekly (or with any regularity), nor was always held on Fridays. Instead, Hardcore Friday was treated more as a separate brand or division, featuring a distinct roster that nonetheless was portrayed by the same five or so wrestlers. It did, however, focus on hardcore wrestling, as opposed to PpW's other rosters/divisions at the time, such as Super Lucha.

Hardcore Friday - along with many other backyard ideas - was dropped once PpW went pro. Years later, in 2024, the concept was resurrected as a series of irregular shows held _mostly_ in [2Koła Motorcycle Pub](@/v/2kola.md) and taking place _mostly_ on Fridays. Like before, the shows prominently feature hardcore wrestling.
