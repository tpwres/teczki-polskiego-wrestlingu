"Hardcore Friday" is a series of shows by [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md) with a long tradition. It started as a "weekly" event during PpW's backyard era, with the word "weekly" being more of a suggestion rather than a rule. Contrary to what the name suggests, the shows weren't necessarily held on Fridays. Instead, Hardcore Friday was treated more as a separate division with a roster consisting of several different characters portrayed by the same five or so wrestlers. It did, however, focus on hardcore wrestling, as opposed to PpW's other rosters/divisions at the time, such as Super Lucha.

Hardcore Friday - along with many other backyard ideas - was dropped once PpW went pro. Years later, in 2024, the old formula was resurrected as a series of irregular shows held (mostly) in [2Koła Motorcycle Pub](@/v/2kola.md) and taking place (mostly) on Fridays. Like before, the shows prominently feature hardcore wrestling.
