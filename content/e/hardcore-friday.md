"Hardcore Friday" is a series of shows by [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md) with a long tradition. It started as a "weekly" event during PpW's backyard era (with the word "weekly" being more of a suggestion rather than a rule) and as its name suggests, it was held on Fridays and featured hardcore wrestling.

Hardcore Friday - along with many other backyard ideas - was dropped once PpW went pro. In 2024, however, the old formula was resurrected as a series of irregular shows held (mostly) in [2Koła Motorcycle Pub](@/v/2kola.md). Once again, the events take place (mostly) on Fridays, and feature hardcore wrestling.
