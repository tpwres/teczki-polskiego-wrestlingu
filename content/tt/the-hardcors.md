The Hardcors (also spelled: The Hardcores) are a Tag Team of [Johnny Blade](@/w/johnny-blade.md) and [Biesiad Strong](@/w/biesiad.md) active in [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md). 

## History

### PpW Ewenement Wrestling (2024-)

Though they have teamed up on separate occasions, Biesiad and Blade did not become a proper tag team until [Miasto Bezprawia](@/e/ppw/2024-02-10-ppw-miasto-bezprawia.md), where they defeated Rude Diabły (roughly: _The Redhead Devils) - the short-lived alliance of [Chris X](@/w/chris-x.md) & [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md). 
While The Hardcors did not compete in proper tag-team competition, they remained allies, establishing _Zmowa przeciwko [Zmowie_](@/tt/zmowa.md).
Their next tag-team appearance was in [2025](@/e/ppw/2025-01-25-ppw-gruba-przesada.md), when they saved [Axel Fox](@/w/jakub.md) from the beatdown by Zmowa, who failed to recruit him.
