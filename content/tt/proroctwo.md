Proroctwo (lit. "The Prophecy") are a Polish villainous group active in [Prime Time Wrestling](@/o/ptw.md), initially founded by [Damian Lambert](@/w/damian-lambert.md) and currently consisting of [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md), [Schwarzmann](@/w/sawicki.md), [Syriusz Dziedzic](@/w/dziedzic.md) and Valentina Vex.

## History

### Prime Time Wrestling (2026-)

#### The First Prophet

At [Odrodzenie](@/e/ptw/2026-01-31-ptw-odrodzenie.md) [Damian Lambert](@/w/damian-lambert.md) debuted a new persona, preaching that a mysterious "Prophet" who knows all the answers would come to PTW. Later at the show, Lambert interfered in a post-match altercation between [Bumper](@/w/fabian.md) and [Dropper](@/w/whiteblade.md). Driving Bumper away, he offered Dropper a lucha mask. Not accepting Dropper's rejection, Lambert laid him out with a German Suplex, forced the mask onto his head and carried Dropper out of the arena.

At [World Cup](@/e/ptw/2026-02-21-ptw-world-cup.md) Lambert debuted a new theme and gave a rambling promo before his match against [Bart Petro](@/w/bart-petro.md). He talked about "multiple voices", the uncertainty of today's world and the need to have a guiding figure. It was met with "idź się leczyć" (roughly _get your head checked_) chants from the crowd, to which Lambert replied that the fans were the ones that need professional help. He announced the Prophet who would guide everyone, and revealed that it was himself. He prophesied the end of the world and promised to save us - just like he'd saved a person so lost that even his name was Dropper. Dropper - now renamed [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md) - made a full entrance wearing a lucha mask gifted him by Lambert, complete with new ring gear. Whiteblade made a quiet exit from the ring and Lambert defeated Petro in the scheduled match.

Some time after [More Moné, More Problems](@/e/ptw/2026-03-29-ptw-more-mone-more-problems.md), Prophet Lambert quietly [left PTW](@/a/ptw-exits.md).

#### Tag Team

At [Krew Pot i Łzy](@/e/ptw/2026-05-23-ptw-krew-pot-i-lzy.md), [Sawicki](@/w/sawicki.md) helped Whiteblade in defeating [Polski Niedźwiedź](@/w/polski-niedzwiedz.md), seemingly joining Proroctwo.

At [Ślepa Furia](@/e/ptw/2026-07-18-ptw-slepa-furia.md), Sawicki returned to PTW. Appearing together with Whiteblade as part of Proroctwo, he debuted a new persona, wearing white fur robe, paint covering half of his face and new music (Rammstein's "Engel"). Before the scheduled match, he gave the audience his "confession" declaring that he threw away his weak old self, promised to stop speaking forever and announced the debut of his new, scary and strong name. Sawicki mentioned that [Prorok Lambert](@/w/damian-lambert.md) had been a false prophet, but the prophecy was intact and the end of the world was still coming.

Afterwards, [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md) and [Sawicki](@/w/sawicki.md) took on and defeated a tag team of [El Hijo del G](@/w/el-g.md) & [El Clavo](@/w/el-clavo.md). 
At [Droga Wojownika](@/e/ptw/2026-09-19-ptw-droga-wojownika.md) the duo won the [PTW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ptw-tag-team-championship.md) #1 Contendership.

#### Group

At [Duch Przeszłości](@/e/ptw/2026-10-31-ptw-duch-przeszlosci.md) the faction underwnt major changes. Sawicki debuted his promised, scary persona, changing his name to Schwarzmann and further changing his look - full facepaint, and a tattered hooded robe with a prison-like jumpsuit underneath.

[Syriusz Dziedzic](@/w/dziedzic.md) made his surprise return to help them snatch the titles from the reigning Champions, [Miyagi Sigma](@/tt/miyagi-sigma.md), in a Horror Match. After the match, he revealed his identity and celebrated with new champs, helping them beat down the former Champions and the unlucky referee Klaudiusz.

After brutalizing Miyagi Shida with multiple Swanton Bombs from Whiteblade, they put him in the casket. As the team turned their focus to Sigma Boy xD, they were interrupted by Sigma's Lucky 8 pupil adeptka Paula, who pleaded for his safety. The villains left the ring and allowed Paula to help Sigma to his feet - only for her to low-blow him and hit a Canadian Destroyer, thus turning heel. Paula embraced Schwarzmann and left the ringside together with other members of the group, showing her allegiance. Thus, Proroctwo became a 4-person group overnight.
