[Chemik](@/w/chemik.md) & [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) are a tag team in [Kombat Pro Wrestling](@/o/kpw.md).

## History

### Kombat Pro Wrestling (2026-)

Prior to [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) [Chemik](@/w/chemik.md) was given a title match against the reigning Champions, [Team Visegrad](@/tt/team-visegrad.md), but no partner. His initial idea was to team up with his former tag team partner, [Eryk Lesak](@/w/eryk-lesak.md), but this proved impossible as Lesak was already booked to defend his [KPW OldTown Championship](@/c/kpw-old-town-championship.md) at that event. Chemik failed to find anybody who would work with him and so was prepared to fight for the titles by his lonesome. Mere minutes before the match began, however, Chemik was unexpectedly joined in the ring by [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md), who would rather team up with his second least favourite member of the KPW roster (the first being [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md)), that allow him to claim both title belts for himself. An uneasy alliance was thus born and despite personal animosities, the duo managed to win the match and become the new KPW Tag Team Champions.
