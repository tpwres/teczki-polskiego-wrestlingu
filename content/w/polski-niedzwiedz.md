Polski Niedźwiedź (_Polish Bear_) is a Polish wrestler working for [Prime Time Wrestling](@/o/ptw.md).

## Professional wrestling career

### Prime Time Wrestling (2025-present)

#### Debut and losing streak

In late December 2024 Polish Bear started publishing a series of vignettes teasing his in-ring debut for [Prime Time Wrestling](@/o/ptw.md). It eventually concluded at [Wiosenna Bijatyka](@/e/ptw/2025-03-15-ptw-wiosenna-bijatyka.md) where he debuted in a Singles Match against [Puncher](@/w/puncher.md). He was however quickly squashed by the reigning [PTW Champion](@/c/ptw-championship.md). The next highlight of his career came when he intervened in a brawl between [Miyagi Shida](@/w/miyagi-shida.md) and [Serg Sullivan](@/w/serg-sullivan.md), but was famously knocked out by Sullivan. This resuled in a brief feud between the two that ended at [Nietykalni](@/e/ptw/2025-07-19-ptw-nietykalni.md) with Serg's victory. From that point on, Bear worked mostly as enhancement talent for PTW, amassing a streak of defeats.

#### Synowie Lasu

At [Świąt Nie Będzie](@/e/ptw/2025-12-13-ptw-swiat-nie-bedzie.md) Polski Niedźwiedź finally managed to break his losing streak by defeating [Baron Wolfgang Storm](@/w/baron-wolfgang-storm.md). In a post-match promo he announced that following the New Year he would come back with a companion. The identy of his partner was revealed at [Odrodzenie](@/e/ptw/2026-01-31-ptw-odrodzenie.md), where he formed a Tag Team with the debuting [Misiek](@/w/misiek.md), another wrestler portraying a bear character. The team was named "Synowie Lasu" (_Sons of the Forest_). After securing a three-win streak right from his debut against teams such as Bump & Drop, Cheeki Breeki and The Polish Stand, the bear duo faced - and lost to - [Miyagi Sigma](@/tt/miyagi-sigma.md) in a #1 Contenders Tag Team Match for the [PTW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ptw-tag-team-championship.md) at [Koloseum](@/e/ptw/2026-04-18-ptw-koloseum.md). 

Later on, Synowie Lasu separated for a couple of shows to perform in singles competition. Polski Niedźwiedź was defeated by wrestlers such as [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md) and [Vincent Caravaggio](@/w/vincent-caravaggio.md). He even challenged [Spartan](@/w/spartan.md) for the [PTW Championship](@/c/ptw-championship.md). During Misiek's match against [Nano Lopez](@/w/nano-lopez.md) for the [PTW Underground Championship](@/c/ptw-underground-championship.md) at [Duch Przeszłości](@/e/ptw/2026-10-31-ptw-duch-przeszlosci.md), Niedźwiedź stopped him while he tried to perform the Package Piledriver on Nano, which cost him the match. Despite that, the duo reconciled and made the decision to return to Tag Team competition.

## International wrestling career

In 2025 Polski Niedźwiedź made a one-off appearance in Italian BestYa Slam federation, where he took part in a Battle Royal Match.
