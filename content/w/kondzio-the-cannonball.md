Kondzio the Cannonball is a Polish rookie wrestler, best known for his appearances in [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md) and [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md).

## Professional wrestling career

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Kondzio began his professional wrestling journey in 2026 when he joined [Ewenement Dojo / Warszawska Szkoła Wrestlingu](@/o/dojo.md) in 2026. 

He made his first public appearance at [Dojo Showcase #2](@/e/frpw/2026-07-19-frpw-dojo-showcase-2.md) where he was announced as new stablemate and manager of [Boro](@/w/boro.md) & [Mutant](@/w/mutant.md) Tag Team. At the same show he proved himself as a valid asset to the group, as he helped his teammates defeat [Zmowa](@/tt/zmowa.md) in a Tag Team Match.

### PpW Ewenement (2026-present)

#### Debut and feud with Rodzina

Kondzio the Cannonball made his first official appearance for [PpW](@/o/ppw.md) at their show made in cooperation with [Wixapol](@/e/ppw/2026-08-15-ppw-wixapol.md). With [Boro](@/w/boro.md) as his manager, he challenged [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md) for a match. Despite massive physical advantage on Isnorr's side, Kondzio proved resilient and innovative, and managed to score the pin via a roll-up.

Later on Kondzio continued to make semi-regular appearances as Boro & Muti's manager. At [Chaos Na Rewirach](@/e/ppw/2026-09-26-ppw-chaos-na-rewirach.md) Boro & Muti defeated Isnorr and [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md) and later intervened in a [PpW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ppw-tag-team-championship.md) Match between [Oskar Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md) and [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md), causing them to lose their Championship to [The Hardcors](@/tt/the-hardcors.md). As revenge for intervening in their match, Rodzina's patriarch Vic Golden ordered Oskar and Filip to abduct [Mutant](@/w/mutant.md). Mutant happened to avoid the beatdown and kidnapping, as he went to answer nature's call at just the right moment. The violent goon duo therefore decided to [abduct Kondzio][kondzio-porwanie] instead.

At [Brutality](@/e/ppw/2026-10-10-ppw-brutality.md) the hostage Kondzio has been used as a bait to lure Mutant and Boro away from winning the TLC Match for the Tag Team Championship. After [Rodzina's](@/tt/rodzina.md) victory, Kondzio managed to [escape][kondzio-porwanie] his kidnappers.
