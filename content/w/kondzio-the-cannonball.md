Kondzio the Cannonball is a Polish rookie wrestler, best known for his appearances in [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md) and [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md). As of 2026, Kondzio is training with [Ewenement Dojo](@/o/dojo.md) and appearing in mostly non-wrestling roles, associating himself with [Boro](@/w/boro.md) and [Mutant](@/w/mutant.md).
