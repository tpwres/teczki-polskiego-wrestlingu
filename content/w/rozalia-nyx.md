Rozalia Nyx is a Polish female wrestler and valet, best known for her appearances in [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md) and [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md).

## Professional wrestling career

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Rozalia started her wrestling career by joining [Ewenement Dojo / Warszawska Szkoła Wrestlingu](@/o/dojo.md) in 2025. She made her first official appearance in Next Big Thing Battle Royale at [FRPW's](@/o/frpw.md) [first Dojo Showcase](@/e/dojo/2026-05-03-dojo_frpw-showcase.md). At [second Dojo Showcase](@/e/frpw/2026-07-19-frpw-dojo-showcase-2.md) few months later she was introduced as [Olgierd's](@/w/olgierd.md) partner and manager.

### PpW Ewenement (2026-present)

#### Debut and Olgierd's valet

Rozalia Nyx made her first official appearance for [PpW](@/o/ppw.md) in a role of Olgierd's valet from [Hardcore Friday: Żeby Było Ci Dobrze](@/e/ppw/2026-09-04-ppw-hardcore-friday.md) onwards. After Olgierd left [Zmowa](@/tt/zmowa.md) at [Chaos Na Rewirach](@/e/ppw/2026-09-26-ppw-chaos-na-rewirach.md), Rozalia remained by his side. At [Brutality](@/e/ppw/2026-10-10-ppw-brutality.md) she got involved in a match between Olgierd and [Mister Z](@/w/mister-z.md), when Zmowa leader assaulted her, which gave him upper hand in a match against "The Wrestling Hooligan".

Besides working as Olgierd's valet, Rozalia made her in-ring debut for PpW at Brutality, where she entered Queen Jadwiga's Battle Royal Match.
