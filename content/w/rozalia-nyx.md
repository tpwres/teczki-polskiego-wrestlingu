Rozalia Nyx is a Polish female rookie wrestler and valet, best known for her appearances in [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md) and [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md). As of 2026, Rozalia is training with [Ewenement Dojo](@/o/dojo.md) and appearing in wrestling and non-wrestling roles, associating herself with [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md).
