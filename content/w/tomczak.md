Tomczak is a Polish wrestler working for [Kombat Pro Wrestling](@/o/kpw.md), [PpW Ewenement](@/o/ppw.md) and [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md).

## Professional wrestling career

### Kombat Pro Wrestling (2023, 2025-present)

#### Debut and early appearances

Initially working security detail, he made his first in-ring appearance as [Chemik's](@/w/chemik.md) unnamed bodyguard at [Pyrkon 2023](@/e/kpw/2023-06-17-kpw-pyrkon-2023.md), but it wasn't until [Arena 27](@/e/kpw/2025-01-24-kpw-arena-27.md) in January 2025 that he made his proper debut as a monster heel persona, where he focused on working mostly multi-men matches, as well as squashing KPW's rookies.

#### Various storylines

Throughout first half of 2026 Tomczak tried to get himself into title picture: At [Arena 32](@/e/kpw/2026-03-13-kpw-arena-32.md) Tomczak unsucesfully tried to win #1 Contendership for [KPW Championship](@/c/kpw-championship.md) in a Triple Threat Match against [Michał Fux](@/w/michal-fux.md) and victorious [Greg](@/w/greg.md). At [Arena 33](@/e/kpw/2026-04-24-kpw-arena-33.md) he defeated [Oskar Haller](@/w/oskar-haller.md) in Contendership Qualifying Match for Fatal Four Way, which ultimately took place at [Arena 34](@/e/kpw/2026-06-26-kpw-arena-34.md) but once again, Tomczak was not the winner. At [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md) he was defeated by [Leon Lato](@/w/leon-lato.md) in a Dog Collar Match.

### PpW Ewenement Wrestling (2026-present)

Tomczak made a surprise debut for PpW at [Dwa Na Dwa](@/e/ppw/2026-02-28-ppw-dwa-na-dwa.md) in a pre-show tag team match, replacing [Sambor](@/w/sambor.md) who was announced, but did not appear at the show.

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

#### HeelTurn Invitational 26' and formation of PSY.CHO

Tomczak made his debut for [FRPW](@/o/frpw.md) at federation's first [Dojo Showcase](@/e/dojo/2026-05-03-dojo_frpw-showcase.md) in May 2026. Later, he was announced as one of the contestants in HeelTurn Invitational 26' Tournament, where he was eliminated in first round by [Bartosz Plata](@/w/plata.md). Starting from FRPW's show in [Siedlce](@/e/frpw/2026-10-03-frpw-siedlce.md) he started appearaing with [Monster High Suzie](@/w/monster-high-suzie.md) as his manager. This marked the start of new villainous group in FRPW called PSY.CHO, as they were quickly joined by [Stowarzysz](@/w/stowarzysz.md) and debuted at [Na Słowo Honoru](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md) by attacking [Boro](@/w/boro.md), Quentin Carter, [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md) and staff workers in a post-match beatdown.
