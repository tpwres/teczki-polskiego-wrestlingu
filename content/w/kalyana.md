## Professional wrestling career

### Training and first appearances (202?-present)

Kalyana began training with Ewenement Dojo/WSW at some point in 2025 or 2026. She made her first in-ring appearance at [Ewenement Dojo Showcase 2](@/e/dojo/2026-07-19-frpw-dojo-showcase-2.md) in an intergender Mixed Tag Team Match.
