Kalyana is a Ukrainian wrestler best known for working with [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md) and [PpW Ewenement](@/o/ppw.md).

## Professional wrestling career

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Kalyana began training with [Ewenement Dojo/Warszawska Szkoła Wrestlingu](@/o/dojo.md) in early 2026. She made her first in-ring appearance at [Ewenement Dojo Showcase 2](@/e/frpw/2026-07-19-frpw-dojo-showcase-2.md) in an intergender Mixed Tag Team Match. She followed her initial attempt with additional appearances for FRPW following shows, where she mainly worked in a Tag Team division - often being paired with [Max Speed](@/w/max-speed.md). 

At [Na Słowo Honoru](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md) while teaming with Speed she was defeated by Kolektyw: [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md) nad [Gloria](@/w/gloria-g-szek.md). After the match she challenged Gloria for a Singles Match at [Zimny Świat](@/e/frpw/2026-12-06-frpw-zimny-swiat.md), with winner becoming #1 Contender for wXw Academy Championship. 

### PpW Ewenement (2026-present)

Kalyana made her debut at [PpW x Wixapol](@/e/ppw/2026-08-15-ppw-wixapol.md) show in August 2026, where she faced [Gloria G Szek](@/w/gloria-g-szek.md) and [Agentka Agatka](@/w/agentka-agatka.md) in a Triple Threat Match. Later on, at [Brutality](@/e/ppw/2026-10-10-ppw-brutality.md) she won Queen Jadwiga's Battle Royal - the first female-only Battle Royal Match in Polish Wrestling history.

## International wrestling appearances

From August 2026 onwards Kalyana started making ocassional appearances abroad. Most notably working at Dresden Wrestling School dojo shows.
