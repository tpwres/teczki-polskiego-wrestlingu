Tony "The Riverman" Sheen is a Polish wrestler best known for his time in [Maniac Zone Wrestling](@/o/mzw.md), [PpW Ewenement](@/o/ppw.md), [Legacy of Wrestling](@/o/low.md) and [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md). He also worked for multiple backyard federations prior to that.

## Professional wrestling career

### Various backyard promotions (2011-2014)

Over the span of 2010s, Sheen competed for various Polish backyard promotions, including [PXW](@/o/pxw.md), WSW and [WKSW](@/o/wksw.md). He earned the nickname "Aparycja" (_Appearance_), due to his unique look, called out in a [backstage video from Backyardmania IV][bm4].
Per his account, he main-evented the final WKSW show. Most of that promotion's cards and shows are considered lost media.

### Maniac Zone Wrestling (2023-present)

#### Referee Tony

Sheen, not announced by name, refereed two MZW shows in 2023.

#### Riverman

In 2024, Tony made his in-ring debut for the promotion at [No Time To Die](@/e/mzw/2024-10-12-mzw-no-time-to-die.md) as Tony "The Riverman" Sheen, winning a battle royal. Wearing a fisherman's raincoat and using a fishing net as his signature weapon, the Riverman character seems to take sailor culture as an inspiration. He later added Aquaman-inspired boots and tights into his attire. As a character, he likes to incorporate fishing references into his promos. At [MZW Forever](@/e/mzw/2025-03-29-mzw-forever.md), Sheen faced [Disco Pablo](@/w/disco-pablo.md) scoring a win thanks to a distraction by [Taras](@/w/taras.md).

#### Championship pursuit

Before [No Time To Die 2](@/e/mzw/2026-06-20-mzw-forever-2.md), [MZW Champion](@/c/mzw-championship.md) [Matt Buckna](@/w/matt-buckna.md) explained that he wouldn't be able to attend the show and defend his Championship aganist [Gustav Gryffin](@/w/gustav-gryffin.md) due to a recent shoulder injury. Tony Sheen, in his response posted the next day, berated Buckna for not putting in the effort and addressed the federation's management to give him the title match against Gustav Gryffin instead. Soon after the match between Gustav and Tony Sheen was made official. Despite help from [Arek Paterek](@/w/arek-paterek.md), his boss from  [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md), Tony was defeated by Gustav. Gryffin's title reign didn't last long, however, as [Marco Hammers](@/w/marco-hammers.md) used the MZW/PpW Opportunity briefcase won by [Mister Z](@/w/mister-z.md) a few months before. 

During the build-up to [MZW Forever 2](@/e/mzw/2026-06-20-mzw-forever-2.md) Tony challenged Marco Hammers to an MZW Championship match, claiming Marco only managed to win the title because his opponent, Gustav Gryffin, was severely weakened after the fight with Sheen. Sheen was once again defeated, but after his loss he returned to the ring and hit Marco with a steel chair to the back. He then followed it by dropping Marco onto the ring's exposed layer of plywood. Sheen took a microphone and declared he would continue to haunt Marco for as long as needed to get another title shot. Then he snatched the Championship and posed with it over the prone Hammers, making an emphatic statement. Soon after it was confirmed that Marco's neck injury was legitimate, and that he would have to retire from wrestling. 

#### MZW Champion 

[Marco Hammers](@/w/marco-hammers.md), the [MZW Champion](@/c/mzw-championship.md), was forced to vacate his title due to a career-ending neck injury. At [Green Madness II](@/e/mzw/2026-11-20-mzw-green-madness-ii.md) Sheen faced [Disco Pablo](@/w/disco-pablo.md) in a 2 Out of 3 Falls No-DQ Match (in which Marco was the Special Guest Referee), winning the gold. 

### PpW Ewenement (2025-present)

At [Wjazd Na Rewir](@/e/ppw/2025-10-24-ppw-wjazd-na-rewir.md) Tony made his PpW debut as one of the competitors in the Zmowa Recruitment Gauntlet Match, where he faced [Gustav Gryffin](@/w/gustav-gryffin.md) and was quickly defeated by him. From that point on he starded making occassional appearances, mostly as enhancement talent.

### Legacy of Wrestling (2025-present)

#### Debut and joining Biuro Prasowe

In December 2025 Tony Sheen made his debut for [Legacy of Wrestling](@/o/low.md) at [Four The Glory](@/e/low/2025-12-20-low-4.md), facing Disco Pablo in a 12 Weapons of Christmas No-DQ Match. After winning the match, Tony Sheen opened the final present for himself: a T-shirt of [Arek Paterek's](@/w/arek-paterek.md) stable [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md), which he officially joined. Following Sheen's loss to [Marcelito](@/w/marcelito.md) (after a distraction by Disco Pablo) at [Open Season](@/e/low/2026-05-24-low-5.md), the angry Paterek promised revenge. He challenged them both to a six-man tag team match against Biuro Prasowe, at Legacy's upcoming [Enter the Dragon](@/e/low/2026-07-10-low-6.md) show. Tony was joined by newest stable members: [Eryk Lesak](@/w/eryk-lesak.md) and [Bartosz Plata](@/w/plata.md), as they won match against Pablo, Marcelito and [Sambor](@/w/sambor.md). 

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Tony made his first appearance for [FRPW](@/o/frpw.md) at [Na Słowo Honoru](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md), where he teamed up with [Bartosz Plata](@/w/plata.md) to face [Boro](@/w/boro.md) and Quentin Carter.
