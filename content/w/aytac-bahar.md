Aytaç Bahar is a German wrestler, who fights primarily in Berlin-based GWF and Project Nova, but also all over the German scene, including two tag team championship runs with [Joshua Amaru](@/w/joshua-amaru.md) in ERW (Dresden) and Catch Factory (Leipzig).
In Poland, Aytaç occasionally appeared in [PpW Ewenement](@/o/ppw.md) since 2023, both solo and as a team with Joshua Amaru.

## Professional wrestling career

### PpW Ewenement Wrestling (2023-present)

#### Sporadic appearances 

Aytaç debuted in Poland working for Warsaw-based federation [PpW](@/o/ppw.md) in March 2023 during [Piwa I Igrzysk](@/e/ppw/2023-03-11-ppw-piwa-i-igrzysk.md). He defeated [Adam Wong](@/w/adam-wong.md) in the [PpW Championship](@/c/ppw-championship.md) #1 Contendership Quarter Final Match, but he failed to secure victory in the final match at [Mistrzowskie Rozdanie](@/e/ppw/2023-05-06-ppw-mistrzowskie-rozdanie.md).

He returned to PpW almost one year later at [Ewenement Haze](@/e/ppw/2024-04-20-ppw-ewenement-haze.md) as part of Baklava Club. He and [Joshua Amaru](@/w/joshua-amaru.md) faced - and lost to - [Pure Gold](@/tt/pure-gold.md). 

In 2026 Bahar and Amaru faced [Bad Trip](@/tt/bad-trip.md) in a [PpW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ppw-tag-team-championship.md) Match at [Inicjatywa Wrestling](@/e/ppw/2026-06-13-ppw-inicjatywa-wrestling.md).
