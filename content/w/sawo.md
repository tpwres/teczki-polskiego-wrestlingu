## Professional Wrestling Career 

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Sawo made his professional wrestling debut working for [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md) at [Dojo Showcase](@/e/dojo/2026-05-03-dojo_frpw-showcase.md) in May 2026, where he took part in the Next Big Thing Battle Royale. Later, at [Dojo Showcase #2](@/e/frpw/2026-07-19-frpw-dojo-showcase-2.md), he challenged [Warszawska Szkoła Wrestlingu's](@/o/dojo.md) Head Coach [Jacob Crane](@/w/jacob-crane.md) to a match. At [Na Słowo Honoru](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md) he issued an Open Challenge which was anwsered by [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md), who easly defeated him. Later at the same show when Tomczak - along with his stablemate [Stowarzysz](@/w/stowarzysz.md) - attacked [Bartosz Plata](@/w/plata.md) and other contestants of the Tag Team Match, Sawo rushed to help, but his aid was not enough to fend off new monster stable called PSY.CHO. This led to the duo of Sawo & Plata called "Bracia Puro" (_Puro Brothers_) facing PSY.CHO at [FRPW's show in Krasnystaw](@/e/frpw/2026-11-28-frpw-krasnystaw.md).
