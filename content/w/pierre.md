## Professional wrestling career

### Prime Time Wrestling (2026)

#### Rookie pre-debut, Lucky Eight

Pierre first appeared in PTW programming at [Koloseum](@/e/ptw/2026-04-18-ptw-koloseum.md). Posing as a regular audience member, Piotrek, he was squashed by [Damian Adler](@/w/damian-adler.md).
At [Dziedzictwo](@/e/ptw/2026-06-27-ptw-dziedzictwo.md) [Arkadiusz Pawłowski](@/w/pan-pawlowski.md) announced a "Lucky Eight" contest, which would span the next eight weeks and include eight rookies paired with more experienced PTW wrestlers. Piotrek was paired with [Fabian](@/w/fabian.md), who started to repackage his mentee in his own image, a flamboyant _Pierre_. During the contest, Fabian and his unwilling apprentice got into a feud with [Daniel Razor](@/w/daniel-razor.md) and his trainee, [Kalom](@/w/kalom.md).

At [Szczere Złoto](@/e/ptw/2026-08-28-ptw-szczere-zloto.md) the results of the Lucky 8 contest were revealed. As it turned out to be a draw, Kalom, Pierre and Gwiezdny Marek were all drafted into the roster and earned their spots in the Gold Rush Rumble match, but neither managed to score a win.

At [Duch Przeszłości](@/e/ptw/2026-10-31-ptw-duch-przeszlosci.md) the duo of Pierre and Fabian faced Kalom and Razor in a losing effort. Fed up with his mentee's perceived incompetence, Fabian attacked him and left the ring, effectively breaking the duo. Pierre then moved his focus to singles competition as a babyface.
