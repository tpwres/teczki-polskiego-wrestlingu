## Professional wrestling career

### Prime Time Wrestling (2026-present)

#### Debut and Synowie Lasu

Misiek (roughly _Teddy_, as in a teddy bear) debuted at [PTW Odrodzenie](@/e/ptw/2026-01-31-ptw-odrodzenie.md) as a bear-themed powerhouse worker. He formed a tag team called Synowie Lasu (_The Sons of the Forest_) with another bear character, [Polski Niedźwiedź](@/w/polski-niedzwiedz.md). After securing a three-win streak right from his debut against teams such as Bump & Drop, Cheeki Breeki and The Polish Stand, the bear duo faced - and lost to - [Miyagi Sigma](@/tt/miyagi-sigma.md) in a #1 Contenders Tag Team Match for the [PTW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ptw-tag-team-championship.md) at [Koloseum](@/e/ptw/2026-04-18-ptw-koloseum.md). 

Later on, Synowie Lasu separated for a couple of shows to perform in singles competition. Misiek defeated wrestlers such as [Sawicki](@/w/schwarzmann.md) and [Whiteblade](@/w/whiteblade.md). He even challenged [Spartan](@/w/spartan.md) and [Nano Lopez](@/w/nano-lopez.md) for the [PTW Championship](@/c/ptw-championship.md) and the [PTW Underground Championship](@/c/ptw-underground-championship.md) respectively, but both attempts proved unsucessful.
