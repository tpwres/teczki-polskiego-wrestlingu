## Professional wrestling career 

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Stowarzysz made his debut for [FRPW](@/o/fprw.md) under alias "adept Maciej" at [Dojo Showcase #1](@/e/dojo/2026-05-03-dojo_frpw-showcase.md) in May 2026, where he entered Next Big Thing Battle Royal. At [Wrestling w Twoim Mieście: Warka](@/e/frpw/2026-10-18-frpw-warka.md) Stowarzysz's gimmick started to take shape, as he entered the ring with [Monster High Suzie](@/w/monster-high-suzie.md) as his manager, seemingly turining him into villainous powerhouse. At [Na Słowo Honoru](@/e/frpw/2026-11-08-frpw-na-slowo-honoru.md) he and Suzie were joined by fellow powerhouse villain - [Tomczak](@/w/tomczak.md) - to form new stable called "PSY.CHO". They officially introduced themselves to the public by viciously battering competitors of [Boro](@/w/boro.md) & Quentin Carter vs [Biuro Prasowe](@/tt/biuro-prasowe.md) Tag Team Match, as well as some of staff workers.
