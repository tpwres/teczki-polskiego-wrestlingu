## Professional wrestling career

### Prime Time Wrestling (2025-2026)

#### Early appearances

Boguś made a handful of in-ring appearances prior to his official debut as one of the rookies facing [Puncher](@/w/puncher.md) in a Three-On-One Handicap Match at [All About The Moné](@/e/ptw/2025-07-27-ptw-all-about-the-mone.md) show. He also served as a referee and timekeeper in a minor storyline where [Arkadiusz Pawłowski](@/w/pan-pawlowski.md) would tease him with promises of a chance to debut in the ring, but always denied it at the last moment. That came to an end during the main event of [Świąt Nie Będzie](@/e/ptw/2025-12-13-ptw-swiat-nie-bedzie.md), where desperate Pawłowski begged Boguś to help Puncher stop [Spartan](@/w/spartan.md). Boguś, however, turned on the leader of [Nietykalni](@/tt/nietykalni.md) and hit a Spear on Puncher to help Spartan secure a victory.

#### Begginings of wrestler career and release

With Pawłowski's regime ended, Boguś finally gained an opportunity to make his official in-ring debut. At [Odrodzenie](@/e/ptw/2026-01-31-ptw-odrodzenie.md) he took part in a Four-Way Match against [Blaze](@/w/blaze.md), Fashion Gabriel and the victorious [Damian Lambert](@/w/damian-lambert.md). Later on, Boguś got involved in short feud with [Baron Wolfgang Storm](@/w/baron-wolfgang-storm.md) during which he faced his nemesis in a Trios Match, where he worked alongside [CJ Klakson](@/w/cj-klakson.md) and [Bart Petro](@/w/bart-petro.md) to defeat Wolfgang, [Damian Adler](@/w/damian-adler.md) and Halfdan. Supposedly there was a plan to grant Boguś One-on-One match against Baron Wolfgang, but the latter one left PTW shortly after aforementioned Trios Match which took place at [Krew, Pot i Łzy](@/e/ptw/2026-05-23-ptw-krew-pot-i-lzy.md). 

At [Dziedzictwo](@/e/ptw/2026-06-27-ptw-dziedzictwo.md) Boguś defeated [Daniel Razor](@/w/daniel-razor.md). After the match he invited Pan Pawłowski to the ring to thank him for all of the advices Pawłowski gave him before a match. This marked Pawłowski's return after a five-month hiatus. Pawłowski, clearly in his old babyface persona, made his entrance and hugged Boguś. Boguś requested Pawłowski's services as his personal manager but before Pawłowski could decide, [Okoński](@/w/lukasz-okonski.md) interrupted and denied him this opportunity, as the Academy and PTW rookies needed Pawłowski more. Pawłowski took the mic, thanked the audience and also decided not to take Boguś's offer, but instead focus on the new generation. 

After brief stint - lasting only [one show](@/e/ptw/2026-08-28-ptw-szczere-zloto.md) - Pawłowski's contract with Prime Time Wrestling was terminated on August 28th, 2026. This caused massive confusion among Polish Wrestling Scene followers, as well among PTW's roster. At the next show - [Droga Wojownika](@/e/ptw/2026-09-19-ptw-droga-wojownika.md) Boguś took part in a Main Event Tag Team Match for [PTW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ptw-tag-team-championship.md), where he entered the ring in a fasion strongly resembling Pan Pawłowski's entrance - including his "signature" knee drop. 

Shortly after Droga Wojownika, on September 22nd, 2026 Boguś announced that he was fired from PTW. In a [explanation video][okon-zwalnia] posted by Prezes Łukasz Okoński it was revealed that the main reason behind his release was his last-minute arrival and skipping - most propably obligatory - creative team meeting and entrances rehearsal before Droga Wojownika show, which caused his Tag Team partner [CJ Klakson](@/w/cj-klakson.md) and opponents - [Miyagi Sigma](@/tt/miyagi-sigma.md) - fury. According to Okoński, the late arrival of Boguś was caused by Arkadiusz Pawłowski, whom Boguś drove to the show. Additionally, both Pawłowski and Boguś tried to sway part of PTW roster to join brand new Pawłowski project.
