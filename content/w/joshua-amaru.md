Joshua Amaru is a German wrestler, mainly appearing for Berlin-based GWF and Project Nova, as well as in other promotions all over the country, where he won two tag team championships in ERW (Dresden) and Catch Factory (Leipzig) with [Aytaç Bahar](@/w/aytac-bahar.md), plus a mixed tag championship together with French wrestler Cory Zero.
In Poland, Joshua appeared occasionally in [PpW Ewenement](@/o/ppw.md) since 2023, both solo and as a tag team with Aytaç Bahar, since 2023.

## Professional wrestling career

### PpW Ewenement Wrestling (2022-present)

#### Sporadic appearances

Joshua Amaru debuted in Poland working for Warsaw-based federation [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md) in November 2022 during [Najlepsza Gala w Mieście](@/e/ppw/2022-11-25-ppw-najlepsza-gala-w-miescie.md), where he lost to Feyyaz Aguila in a Singles Match. Later on he also faced [Bill Feager](@/w/feager.md) in a #1 Contendership Quarter Final Match for the [PpW Championship](@/c/ppw-championship.md) at [Piwa I Igrzysk](@/e/ppw/2023-03-11-ppw-piwa-i-igrzysk.md). He followed up with [Mistrzowskie Rozdanie](@/e/ppw/2023-05-06-ppw-mistrzowskie-rozdanie.md) where he lost to [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md).

He returned to PpW almost one year later at [Ewenement Haze](@/e/ppw/2024-04-20-ppw-ewenement-haze.md), now in a heel role as part of Baklava Club. He and [Aytaç Bahar](@/w/aytac-bahar.md) faced [Pure Gold](@/tt/pure-gold.md) in a losing effort. 

In 2026 Bahar and Amaru faced [Bad Trip](@/tt/bad-trip.md) in a [PpW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ppw-tag-team-championship.md) Match at [Inicjatywa Wrestling](@/e/ppw/2026-06-13-ppw-inicjatywa-wrestling.md), failing to capture the gold.
