Laurance Roman is a German wrestler from Dresden, known to Polish audiences from [Maniac Zone Wrestling](@/o/mzw.md), [PpW Ewenement Wrestling](@/o/ppw.md) and [Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu](@/o/frpw.md).

## Professional wrestling career

### Maniac Zone Wrestling (2015-2018)

Laurance Roman worked for [Maniac Zone Wrestling](@/o/mzw.md) from 2015 until 2018, making a total of three appearances.

### PpW Ewenement (2026-present)

Laurance returned to Poland in 2026, when he and Nick Schreier teamed up against [Rodzina](@/tt/rodzina.md) at [PpW's](@/o/ppw.md) show [Inicjatywa Wrestling](@/e/ppw/2026-06-13-ppw-inicjatywa-wrestling.md). The duo, known as "The Grind", later faced [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md) and [Maverick](@/w/maverick.md) in a Tag Team Match at [Żeby Było Ci Dobrze](@/e/ppw/2026-09-04-ppw-hardcore-friday.md).

### Fundacja Rozwoju Polskiego Wrestlingu (2026-present)

Roman worked for [FRPW](@/o/frpw.md) at their show in [Siedlce](@/e/frpw/2026-10-03-frpw-siedlce.md) where he teamed up with [Stowarzysz](@/w/stowarzysz.md) to defeat [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md) and [Markus Paskal](@/w/markus-paskal.md).
