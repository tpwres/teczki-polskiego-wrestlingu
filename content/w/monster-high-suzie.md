## Professional wrestling career

### Training and first appearances (202?-present)

Zuza began training with Ewenement Dojo/WSW at some point in 2025 or 2026. She made her first in-ring appearance at [Ewenement Dojo Showcase](@/e/dojo/2026-05-03-dojo_frpw-showcase.md) in the Next Big Thing Battle Royal. She followed her initial attempt with four more matches as enhancement talent at [More Brutal Than Metal 2](@/e/ppw/2026-06-04-ppw-mystic-more-brutal-than-metal.md) and then [Dojo Showcase 2](@/e/frpw/2026-07-19-frpw-dojo-showcase-2.md).

She made her first official solo match - dropping the rookie moniker - at [Hardcore Saturday](@/e/ppw/2026-11-07-ppw-hardcore-saturday.md), against [Agentka Agatka](@/w/agentka-agatka.md).
