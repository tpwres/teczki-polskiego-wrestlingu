The [Freebird Rule][freebird-rule] - named after the American tag team [the Fabulous Freebirds][freebirds] - is an angle where three or more members of a team or stable are considered equal Champions, even if they didn't personally contribute to winning a tag team title. This "rule" is also in effect on the Polish scene.

<!-- more -->

## Prime Time Wrestling

### PAKA

[PAKA](@/tt/paka.md) were the first to win the [PTW Tag Team Championship](@/c/ptw-tag-team-championship.md). At [PTW #4: The Mystery](@/e/ptw/2023-06-25-ptw-4-mystery.md) the tag team of [Taras](@/w/taras.md) and [Disco Pablo](@/w/disco-pablo.md) scored a victory and then defended their titles on two more occasions. Although he never found himself competing for the belt himself, [Boro](@/w/boro.md) was also considered a full Champion.

## Kombat Pro Wrestling

### Team Visegrad

[David Oliwa](@/w/david-oliwa.md) and [Ricky Sky](@/w/ricky-sky.md), forming [Team Visegrad](@/tt/team-visegrad.md), won the [KPW Tag Team Championships](@/c/kpw-tag-team-championship.md) at [Godzina Zero 2025](@/e/kpw/2025-08-22-kpw-godzina-zero-2025.md). [Lukáš Mottl](@/w/lukas-mottl.md), the third member of the team, defended the title at [Arena 31](@/e/kpw/2026-01-16-kpw-arena-31.md) (in two matches, back-to-back) and at [Godzina Zero 2026](@/e/kpw/2026-09-11-kpw-godzina-zero-2026.md). The latter event also saw the addition of Johan Otto, replacing Oliwa who was unable to compete due to an injury.

## PpW Ewenement Wrestling

### Zmowa

The heel stable [Zmowa](@/tt/zmowa.md), represented by [Mister Z](@/w/mister-z.md) and [Jakub](@/w/jakub.md), became the inaugural [PpW Tag Team Champions](@/c/ppw-tag-team-championship.md) at [Dwa Na Dwa](@/e/ppw/2026-02-28-ppw-dwa-na-dwa.md). In the title matches to follow, the gold was defended by [Marco Hammers](@/w/marco-hammers.md) & Mister Z, and Marco & Jakub. Similarly to Boro, [Olgierd](@/w/olgierd.md) was also recognised as a Champion, although he never got to defend the title.

### Rodzina

The stable consists of five people - [Vic Golden](@/w/vic-golden.md), [Oskar Alexander](@/w/oskar-alexander.md), [Filip Fux](@/w/filip-fux.md), [Robert Star](@/w/robert-star.md) and [Isnorr](@/w/isnorr.md) - all of whom are recognised as Champions. However, since winning the titles at [Nü Wrestling](@/e/ppw/2026-07-04-ppw-nu-wrestling.md), Vic and Oskar have been the only ones to defend them (as of September 2026).

## Maniac Zone Wrestling

The Freebird Rule was never put in effect throughout the 2.5 years the [MZW Tag Team Championship](@/c/mzw-tag-team-championship.md) was active.
